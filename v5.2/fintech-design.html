What we do

Money makes people careful. A fintech product has to be clear about numbers, honest about risk and fast when it matters — and it has to feel trustworthy from the first screen, often for people using a financial product for the first time.

We have designed for India’s largest stockbroker, one of its biggest banks and a banking-technology platform, among others. That work has taught us to make complex financial information readable without making it less accurate.

What we design for fintech

  1. Investing and trading

    For Groww: a financial news feed, Groww Stories — market events turned into stories a first-time investor can follow — and design across Groww Invest, Groww Credit and Astra.

  2. Financial tools

    Calculators that explain rather than overwhelm — like Groww’s XIRR calculator, redesigned around a projection of the user’s own wealth.

  3. Rewards and commerce

    Layouts for HDFC Bank’s SmartBuy rewards platform, designed and tested to find what worked.

  4. Developer platforms

    A rebuilt documentation portal for Zeta’s Cipher, shaped by extensive user testing.

Making markets readable

As millions of first-time investors entered India’s stock market, Groww needed to make financial news accessible to them. We designed Groww Stories — bite-sized, visual stories of market events, results and IPOs — which now reach 400,000 users a day.

Questions

Which fintech products have you designed?

Investing apps, financial news and calculators for Groww; a rewards platform for HDFC Bank’s SmartBuy; a documentation portal for Zeta’s Cipher. We have also worked with Paytm and Policybazaar.

How do you design for trust?

Clear numbers, plain language, honest states for when things go wrong, and nothing that rushes a decision about money. We test with real users — often first-time users — before anything ships.

Can you work within regulatory and compliance requirements?

Yes. We design with your compliance and legal teams’ requirements from the start, rather than fitting them in at the end.

Do you work with early-stage fintech start-ups?

Yes — from MVP design for a new product through to an embedded design team for an established one.
