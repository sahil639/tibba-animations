What we do

Most products lose people in the same few places: the landing page, the sign-up, the first session, the price. Growth design is the work of finding those places and fixing them — one flow at a time, measured against the numbers that matter to your business.

We start from what your users actually do, not what the funnel diagram says they do. Then we redesign the moments that cost you the most, test the changes, and keep going.

How growth design works

  1. Find the leaks

    01

    Your analytics and an experience audit together show where people drop off, and what it costs you.

  2. Pick the bets

    02

    The changes most likely to move the number, ranked by impact and effort.

  3. Redesign the moments

    03

    Landing pages, onboarding, pricing, checkout — designed for the user’s next step, not the whole product at once.

  4. Measure and repeat

    04

    Each change shipped and measured, the next bet chosen from what we learned.

Results our clients measured

As Shyft & Mindhouse’s support design team for eight months, we redesigned their landing pages, onboarding and therapy flow: direct purchases through the landing page rose 15x, sales team intervention fell 70%, and the therapy flow’s revenue tripled. At Firstpost, a video-first redesign doubled both traffic and revenue.

Questions

What is growth design?

Product design aimed at a business number: sign-ups, activation, purchases, retention. It covers the flows that decide those numbers — landing pages, onboarding, pricing, checkout — and is measured by them.

How soon will we see results?

Each change is designed to be shipped and measured on its own, so you see the effect of the first ones within the engagement, not at the end of it.

Do you need our analytics?

It helps. If you do not have the right events tracked yet, setting them up is part of the first step.

Can you work alongside our product team?

Yes. Most of our growth work is done embedded with a client’s product and engineering teams, on their release cadence.
