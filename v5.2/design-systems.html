What we do

When a product grows quickly, its interface grows in every direction at once. Twelve versions of the same bottom sheet, five shades of the same grey, a button that behaves differently on every screen. Each one slows your designers and developers down a little; together they slow the whole company.

A design system fixes that at the root. We audit what you have, agree the foundations — colour, type, spacing, motion — and build the components your product actually uses, with the variants and documentation your team needs to use them without asking.

How we build a design system

  1. Audit the interface

    Every screen and component in use today, grouped and counted, so the duplicates and the gaps are plain to see.

  2. Agree the foundations

    Colour, type, spacing, grids and motion as tokens, named so designers and developers mean the same thing.

  3. Build the components

    The components your product uses, each with its variants and states, replacing the many one-offs.

  4. Document it

    How and when to use each part, so new designers and developers can work from the system on day one.

  5. Ship it in code

    Production-ready UI handed to your developers, and support while the system goes into the product.

Fewer parts, faster releases

At Shyft & Mindhouse, twelve versions of the bottom sheet and eight of the empty state became one component each, with variants. At Breathe ESG, a design system built from the first day helped their team ship 50% faster.

Questions

How long does a design system take?

A first version usually takes 8–12 weeks, depending on the size of the product. It is never finished: it grows with the product, and we can stay on to look after it.

Do we need one yet?

If your designers redraw the same parts, or your developers rebuild them, yes. The earlier it starts, the less there is to untangle — which is why we put a small one under every MVP.

Will you rebuild our whole product?

No. We start from what you have and replace the one-offs with shared components, screen by screen, without stopping your releases.

Do you work in our tools?

We work in Figma and hand over production-ready UI your developers can ship.

Can our team run it afterwards?

Yes — that is the point. We document every part and hand it over, or stay on as part of your team if you prefer.
